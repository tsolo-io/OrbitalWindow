# OrbitalWindow

Manage GPU-accelerated research/science desktops on Docker. Each user gets their own Linux desktop
(LinuxServer webtop) in the browser. OrbitalWindow is the single login where they start, stop and
open it, and reach their files.

- [Design](design.md): what is being built, in phases.
- [Proof-of-concept learnings](poc-learnings.md): what was verified and why things are the way they are.
- [Architecture Decision Records](adr/index.md): the decisions and their reasons.
