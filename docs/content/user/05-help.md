# Problems and questions

**The desktop does not open, or shows a grey page.** Wait a few seconds after the status says
*Running*, then press **Open desktop** again.

**My desktop was stopped.** An administrator may have stopped it, for example for maintenance. Your
files are safe; press **Start** again.

**My new software is missing.** After **Apply** has finished, stop and start your desktop.

**Windows does not accept my password for the network drive.** Use your OrbitalWindow user name and
password. If you changed your password recently, Windows may remember the old one: in *Credential
Manager*, remove the entry for `{host}`.

**Something else?** Contact your administrator and mention your user name, **{user}**.
